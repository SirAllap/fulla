Your month can start when your salary comes in.

- **Periods start with the salary**, in Settings › Household: pick your salary category and each salary starts a period, whatever day it arrives. Paid early on the 26th? The previous period ends on the 25th. Paid late? The period stays open until you add it, and whatever you spent on that day moves with it. A salary a recurring item added on its own counts once you save it with its real date. After 35 days without one, the overview asks whether it came in.

This needs a small database update: Fulla offers it at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
