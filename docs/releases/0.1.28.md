Adding a fixed cost is easier.

- **Categories look like the Add screen.** In the fixed cost form, categories are the same tiles with their icon and colour, the ones you use most first, with **More** for the rest and the subcategories right below the one you pick. Nothing was removed: every category and subcategory can still be chosen.
- **A summary before you save.** The form ends with one line saying what it will do, for example "Monthly on day 2 · Cash · until 27 Mar · writes itself".
- **It tells you what is missing.** When Save is greyed out, the form says why: "Still needed: Name · Amount · Category".
- Everything else in the form is as it was: the account, how often, the months, when it ends, "Write itself down", and Delete.

Nothing to update in the database.
