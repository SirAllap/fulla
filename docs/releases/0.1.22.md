Quarterly payments, payments in the months you choose, and fixed costs while you wait for your salary.

- **Repeat every 2, 3 or 6 months.** A monthly item can now repeat every few months, counting from the month you name as the first payment: taxes, insurance, a quarterly fee.
- **Only in the months you pick.** For an irregular calendar, such as October and December, or January, April, July and October, choose the months and the day. Items that write themselves do so in exactly those months.
- **Fixed costs while your period is open.** When your salary is late and the period runs past its usual length, the overview no longer hides everything: the fixed costs are still listed, and the forecast says it is waiting for your next salary instead of disappearing.
- **Fixed costs in Insights** as well, with the same total, what is charged and what is to come, and each charge.

Update database first: Settings › About › Check now › Update now (a few lines of SQL, safe to run twice). Then update every phone that shares the household: a phone on an older version would treat an item with chosen months as if it fell due every month.
