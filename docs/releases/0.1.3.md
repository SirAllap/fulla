Fulla now asks for your language first.

- The very first time you open Fulla, it shows the six languages it speaks: English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano and Português. The one your phone uses is already picked.
- You can change the language later in **Settings › Appearance › Language**, on every Android version (before, only Android 13 and later had it).

On 0.1.2? Fulla offers this at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
