Trips and events with their own budget.

- **Plan a trip.** In **Settings › Trips**, tap **New trip**: a name, the dates and, if you like, a budget. For example: Porto, 12 to 19 August, 300 €.
- **Spend on it.** While the trip is on, new expenses go to it by themselves. You can change that in the entry's details.
- **See how it goes.** The trip has its own jar: what you've spent, what's left, and how much you can spend per day. The Overview shows it while the trip is on.
- **Your month stays true.** Trip spending always counts in the month. It doesn't eat your category budgets, unless you switch **Count in monthly budgets** on.
- Phones still on an older version keep the trip of every expense they didn't change.

**Using your own Supabase project?** This version adds to the database. Run the new `setup.sql` from this release in your project's SQL Editor, as before: it applies only what is new.

On 0.1.5? Fulla offers this at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
