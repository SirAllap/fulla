Trips, made clearer.

- **Who's going.** A trip now has its travellers. Only their new expenses go to the trip by themselves; everyone else's stay everyday expenses.
- **A clear choice before you save.** While a trip is on, the Add screen shows **Everyday | the trip** above the amount. After saving, Fulla says where it went.
- **Delete a trip.** In the trip's menu. Its expenses stay, as everyday expenses.
- **A kind for each trip:** holiday, work, event, visiting family or other, each with its own icon.

**Using your own Supabase project?** Run the new `setup.sql` from this release in your project's SQL Editor, as before.

On 0.1.7? Fulla offers this at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
