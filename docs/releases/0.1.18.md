Subcategories, one more level, and dialogs that fit every phone.

- **Subcategories**: in Settings › Categories, add subcategories to a category (Pets › Vet, Food). When writing a row down, pick the category and its subcategories appear just below; "General" keeps it in the category itself.
- **One more level**: a list field can be asked only in some categories (Settings › Fields › Ask only in), right under them: Pets › Vet › who it was for. The overview opens a category into its subcategories, and a subcategory into each value of that field, down to the rows.
- On phones with the three-button navigation bar, Save and Cancel sat under the bar in taller dialogs. Every dialog now keeps clear of the system bars and the keyboard, with room above and below; when there is more than fits, the content scrolls and the buttons stay in view. Bottom sheets scroll too.

This needs a small database update: Fulla offers it at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
