A forecast you can check, and your fixed costs in view.

- **By the end of the period** replaces "Heading for". It says what you would spend and what you would keep, as a range that narrows with the days, and a tap shows how it is worked out line by line: income, spent so far, fixed costs still to charge, everyday spending still to come.
- **Fixed costs are exact.** The forecast reads the dates and amounts of your recurring items that write themselves, so a new car or bike payment counts from the day you create it, not from the day it is first charged.
- **Everyday spending is learned from your last three periods**, from this day on, adjusted to how this one is going. A single odd period hardly moves it, and trips and recurring items are left out. With no earlier period to learn from, Fulla says so instead of guessing.
- **Fixed costs** has its own section on the overview: the total, what is charged and what is still to come, what your income leaves after them, what you can spend per day meanwhile, and each charge with its day, on a sheet of liquid to scale.
