Updating is no longer a hunt, financing you can follow, fixed costs that explain themselves, and categories you can delete.

- **A new version tells you itself.** The app asks GitHub every time it is opened and once an hour while it stays open (one small request). When there is a new version a pop-up says so, in whatever screen you are on, with what it brings and an **Update now** button. If you tap **Not now** it stays visible: the dot on the gear, and a banner at the top of Settings with the button; the pop-up comes back once a day until you update.
- **Check for updates now, at the bottom of Settings.** No need to go to About and back: the button is at the end of the list, and when it finds a new version the update sheet opens on the spot (it also works from About).
- **The forecast numbers say what they are made of.** A line under "At the end of the period" says which day of the period it is and that the figures are an estimate (what is already spent, plus the fixed costs still to come, plus your usual everyday spending for the days left), and warns when it is still early and the range is wide. The tile that said "After your fixed costs you keep" took the income minus the fixed costs only, so it ignored what you had already spent and did not agree with "You can spend per day". It is now **Left to spend**: income − spent − fixed costs still to come, spelled out on the tile, and "per day" is exactly that divided by the days left.
- **The bars of the fixed costs count down.** On the overview, the bar of a pending charge fills as its day comes closer, measured over the length of the period (a charge 11 days away in a 31-day period is about a third short of full). Once it is paid the bar is full and changes colour, so what is already applied to your income is told apart from what is still to come. A line under the title says so, and the "Still to come" tile no longer shows a full glass when nothing has been paid yet.
- **Each charge says when and which.** "Due Tue 13 Oct · in 11 d", and, for a financing, "Instalment 1 of 3" on its own line so nothing is cut short. In Settings › Fixed costs it counts what is paid: "0 of 3 paid".
- **Analysis is a button, and the gear stays put.** The three dots of the overview only held Analysis, so they are gone: Analysis is a bigger button on the left of the top bar (only its icon on a narrow phone). The gear is now the last thing on the right of every tab; History's own menu (import, export) moved to the left of the sync icon so it no longer pushes the gear aside.
- **Delete a category or a subcategory.** Open it in Settings › Categories and tap **Delete**. If entries use it you choose the category they move to (a subcategory's own category is already picked) and they move together with the deletion. A category nothing refers to any more leaves the list. If a budget, a fixed cost or a field still uses it, the dialog says so, and it stays in the list, archived. A category with subcategories asks you to delete or archive them first.

Nothing to update in the database.
