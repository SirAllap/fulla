A guide for your first minutes with Fulla.

- **When does your month start?** New households are asked first. Choose the 1st, your payday, or the 1st with your salary counting for the next month, and see right away which days "this month" covers. No more salary from the 28th landing in the wrong month.
- **What's in your accounts today?** Set the starting balance of each account, so Balances begins where your bank is. You can also edit it later in Settings.
- **Lock Fulla?** Turn on the fingerprint or screen lock from the start, if your phone has one.
- **A short tour** points at the real app: the jar, the tabs, the keypad and the gear. Every step can be skipped.
- Already using Fulla? Nothing changes for you. Open it any time from **Settings › Show the guide again**.
- Fixed: in a household kept only on the phone, some month settings could make the app crash.

On 0.1.4? Fulla offers this at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
