The first public release.

Fulla keeps a household's money straight: what comes in, what goes out, and what stays. For one person or a whole household, offline first.

**Install:** download `fulla-0.1.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser or file manager. From now on the app finds new versions here and updates itself (Settings › About).

**Start:** on one phone right away, or shared with your household in about five minutes with a free Supabase project the app sets up from a token. The README's [Get started](https://github.com/SirAllap/fulla#get-started) walks through it.

What is in it:

- Expenses, income, refunds, transfers between your own accounts, and settlements between people.
- Splits (equal, by shares, by amounts), per-person balances and the fewest payments that settle them.
- Budgets, recurring items, custom fields, tags, subcategories, months that start on payday.
- Import from a bank or another app: CSV (columns guessed in six languages), OFX and QIF.
- Invite by QR code; sync that works offline and settles conflicts the same way on every phone.
- Backups to a file, CSV export.
- English, Spanish, French, German, Italian and Portuguese.

`setup.sql` is only needed to set up a Supabase project by hand.
