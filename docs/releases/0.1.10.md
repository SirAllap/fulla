Starting balances, made clear.

- **In your accounts.** Once you've set a starting balance on an account, the Overview shows what your accounts hold today: the starting balance, plus what came in, minus what went out. Tap it to see each account.
- The **Opening balance** field now says what it is: money already in the account when you start, not income.

On 0.1.9? Fulla offers this at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
