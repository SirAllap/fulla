Fixed costs that end: financing, instalments, anything with a last payment.

- **A fixed cost can now say when it ends.** In Fixed costs, **Ends** has three choices: *Never* (as before), *On a date*, or *After some payments*. Pick "6 payments" for a six-month financing and the fixed cost writes six and stops; the last one is the end.
- **Any rhythm works.** Monthly, every two or three months, only the months you pick, weekly, yearly or daily: the end applies to all of them, because it counts the payments the fixed cost itself would write.
- **You see what you are setting before you save it.** "Last payment: 27 Mar · 6 payments in all". If the end leaves no payment at all (it comes before the first), the screen says so and does not save it.
- **Progress in the list.** A fixed cost with an end shows "payment 3 of 6", and "Ended 27 Mar" once it is done. It is not deleted: what it wrote stays in History.
- Nothing changes for the fixed costs you already have: without an end they go on exactly as before.
