Mark the salary that starts your month.

- **Starts the month** is now a switch on the income itself, not a category in Settings, so a second salary in the same category never moves your month. Fulla turns it on for an income that looks like your last salary (same category and account), and when a salary arrives without it, from an import for example, the overview asks whether it is yours.
- The period still waiting for its salary reads **From Fri 28 Aug · open**, instead of an end date it cannot know yet.

This needs a small database update: Fulla offers it at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
