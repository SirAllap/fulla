For households that share all their money.

- **One shared pot.** In **Settings › Household › Money between members**, choose **One shared pot**. From then on, everything belongs to the household: cash or bank, expenses or income. It doesn't matter who adds what, and nobody owes anybody. Balances shows your accounts instead of debts.
- If something was owed from before, Fulla asks what to do with it: **Settle it now** clears it, **Keep it** leaves it for later.
- When you invite someone to a household, Fulla now asks how you handle money together.
- Phones still on an older version follow the shared pot too: the server makes sure their new entries create no debt.

**Using your own Supabase project?** This version adds to the database. Run the new `setup.sql` from this release in your project's SQL Editor, the same way as the first time: it applies only what is new. Until then, the shared pot can't be switched on.

On 0.1.3? Fulla offers this at the top of Settings, or tap **Settings › About › Check now**, then **Update now**.
