Choose the account of a fixed cost.

- **A fixed cost can be charged to any of your accounts.** Until now every fixed cost went to the first account, with no way to change it. Adding or editing a fixed cost now shows **Account** (when you have more than one) and the rows it writes go there. The rent to one account, the gym to another.
- A fixed cost you already have keeps the account it has; change it by editing the fixed cost. What it has already written stays where it is: only the charges written from then on go to the new account.

Nothing to update in the database.
