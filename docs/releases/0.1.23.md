The calendar of a repeating payment, in view.

- **Every 2, 3 or 6 months now lights up every month it falls due.** Pick January for "every 2 months" and January, March, May, July, September and November are highlighted, so you see the whole calendar of the payment before saving. Tap another month to move the start.
- **The coming year of a repeating payment, in plain dates.** Under the months, a line lists when the payment falls due over the next year ("15 Sep 2030 · 15 Dec 2030 · 15 Mar 2031"), so you know exactly what you are setting before you save.
- **Only from today on.** "Every 3 months from September" starts with the next September, December, March… that is still ahead, and never charges a day of this month that has already gone by.
