Fixed costs that really write themselves.

- **Fixed costs now write themselves in households that live only on your phone.** Until now only shared households did, so a rent or a subscription set to "Write itself down" never showed up in History and the total never went down. They are written the day they come due, dated on their day, like any expense you had written by hand. If you did not open the app that day, they are written when you open it.
- **When a fixed cost is saved, what has already come this period is written at once.** Create the rent on the 2nd and the rent of the 1st is in History straight away. Before you save, the screen tells you what it is about to write ("Saving writes it now: 1 Oct") and, for repeats every few months, the dates of the coming year.
- **No double charges.** If you had already written the rent by hand, the fixed cost recognises it (same category, about the same amount, within three days of its day) and does not write it again. The forecast counts it as paid, no longer as still to come.
- **A fixed cost starts with the current period.** A new one, or one made before this version, does not reach back into periods you are not looking at. What fell due before and was never written is offered in Fixed costs (the overview mentions it): write it, or let it go. Resumed, or moved to another day, a fixed cost applies from today on.
- **Overdue charges can be applied by hand.** If a charge's day has come and nothing wrote it, Fixed costs shows it as overdue with an **Apply** button.
- Fixed costs says whether a charge was written by Fulla ("Charged on…") or by you ("Written by hand…").
